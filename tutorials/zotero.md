[Zotero](https://www.zotero.org/) is a fantastic bibliography and citation manager that is free, flexible, and intuitive. It allows customization and group collaboration.

1.  Go to the [registration](https://www.zotero.org/user/register) page and sign up for a free account.

2.  Next, [download](https://www.zotero.org/download/) the following:

    - Standalone **Zotero app** for your computer

    - **Zotero Connector** for your internet browser.

3.  Install the Zotero app on your computer and then make the following changes in the app's settings (to get to settings on a Mac, select **Zotero → Settings** in the menu bar (or press and hold the ⌘, keys on your keyboard with the app open); to get to settings on a PC, select **Edit → Settings**). 

4.  In the **Sync** tab under **Settings**, enter your username and password (this will ensure that you can access your Zotero library both from your computer and online).

5.  In the **Cite** tab under **Styles**, ensure that the following three styles are installed (if they are not, click **Get additional styles...** and install them):

    - Chicago Manual of Style 18th edition (notes and bibliography)

    - Chicago Manual of Style 18th edition (shortened notes and bibliography)

    - Chicago Manual of Style 18th edition (author-date)

6.  In the **Cite** tab under **Word Processor Plugins**, click **Install Microsoft Word Add-in** (if it says **Reinstall Microsoft Word Add-in**, the add-in is already installed). Once the process finishes, restart Microsoft Word and ensure that you can now find a new Zotero tab in the Microsoft Word menu bar.

7.  Optional: In the **Advanced** tab under **Files and Folders**, you can change the default directory where your data will be stored (recommended if you wanted to use something like Google Drive to automatically back up your data and PDFs).

8.  Consult the [Zotero Quick Start Guide](https://www.zotero.org/support/quick_start_guide) to explore some of the useful features Zotero offers.
